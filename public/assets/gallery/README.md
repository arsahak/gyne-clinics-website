## Image Guidelines

### Privacy & Consent
- ✅ All images must have explicit patient consent
- ✅ Remove all identifying features (faces, tattoos, unique marks)
- ✅ Comply with GDPR and medical confidentiality regulations
- ✅ Store consent forms separately and securely

### Technical Specifications
- **Format:** JPG (for medical photography)
- **Resolution:** 1200x1600px minimum
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 (portrait) or 4:3 (landscape)
- **File Size:** Keep under 500KB for web performance
- **Color:** sRGB color space
- **Quality:** 85-90% JPEG quality

### Photography Standards
- Consistent lighting (medical lighting preferred)
- Same angle and distance for before/after pairs
- Neutral background (medical blue or gray)
- Clear focus on treatment area
- Professional medical photography equipment

### Before Uploading
1. Verify patient consent is documented
2. Ensure images are properly anonymized
3. Check image quality and clarity
4. Confirm matching pairs (same patient, same angle)
5. Optimize file size without losing quality

## Placeholder Approach

Until real patient images are available, you can:

1. **Use Medical Stock Photos**
   - Purchase from medical stock photo sites
   - Ensure proper licensing for medical use
   - Label clearly as "representative images"

2. **Hire Medical Photographer**
   - Work with consent patients
   - Professional medical photography
   - Proper documentation and consent

3. **Display Message**
   - Show "Images available during consultation"
   - Protect patient privacy
   - Build trust through transparency

## Alternative: Private Gallery

Consider implementing a private gallery system where:
- Images are only shown during in-person consultations
- Password-protected online gallery for serious inquiries
- Email-gated access after initial contact

This protects patient privacy while allowing prospective patients to see results.

## Legal Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Patient consent form signed and dated
- [ ] Images properly anonymized
- [ ] GDPR compliance verified
- [ ] Medical photography standards met
- [ ] Storage and access controls in place
- [ ] Regular consent re-verification (annually)
- [ ] Option for patients to revoke consent
